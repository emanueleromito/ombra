# T0.11: compact configurations

- **Commands:** `make bench TASK=t0.11` then `make results-pull`; `make test-gpu` (A10) for the oracle
- **Commit:** `1251d55`
- **GPU:** NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB (timings and errors); NVIDIA A10 (oracle)
- **CUDA:** 12.6.3 image. Compiled for sm_80 and sm_86.
- **OpenFHE:** v1.6.1, openfhe-python 1.6.1.0 (pinned image)
- **Baseline:** `m0_depth2`, unchanged: HEStd_128_classic, ring dimension 16384, depth 2, scaling 50 bits, first modulus 60 bits, batch size 8192, FIXEDMANUAL, HYBRID (decision 0009)
- **Candidate:** `m0_compact_n8192`: HEStd_128_classic, ring dimension 8192, depth 2, scaling 40 bits, first modulus 55 bits, batch size 4096, FIXEDMANUAL, HYBRID, 3 key-switching digits. Q = 55 + 40 + 40 bits, P = one 60-bit prime. **log2(Q) = 135, log2(QP) = 195**, under the 218-bit 128-bit-classic limit at N = 8192; a test asserts this and OpenFHE accepts the level.
- **Raw output:** `docs/plan/results/raw/results/t0.11-a100.json` (gitignored)
- Timings: median of 10 runs after 2 warm-up runs, Release build. Search times are kernel-only (no relinearize or rescale). Seeded synthetic data.

## Outcome

- **Step 1, smaller ring: does not pass.** `m0_compact_n8192` fails the 1e-6 score-error bound at every size, so **no preset is proposed**. It expands about 2.1x faster and has about 40% smaller keys, but it does not save storage per vector: both presets have 3 towers, and bytes per slot are the same.
- **Step 2, compact plaintext storage: works, with costs.** fp16 and int8 cut stored bytes to 1/24 and 1/48 of RNS plaintext (2d and d bytes per vector). At `m0_depth2`, search becomes 8.7x to 9.9x slower than with RNS plaintexts held ready, because encoding on the GPU dominates. Quantization adds a score error far above 1e-6.
- **Step 3:** see `2026-11-09-t0.11-seeded-encryption.md`.

RNS plaintexts double search speed against ciphertext x ciphertext, as each multiply has half the work. For fp16 and int8 the time per pass is about 0.0664 s of encoding and about 0.009 s to 0.012 s of search at `m0_depth2`: encoding is about 85% to 89% of the time. Encoding once and caching RNS plaintexts recovers the speed but loses the storage saving while held on the GPU.

The ciphertext x plaintext kernel matches OpenFHE `EvalMult` + `EvalAdd` exactly when fed OpenFHE-encoded plaintexts. Floating-point encoding cannot be word-identical in general, so the encoder is compared with `MakeCKKSPackedPlaintext`: at most 1 unit of coefficient difference at `m0_depth2` and 0 at the compact preset (stated tolerance 2 units), and decoded slots within 1.4e-13 and 1.1e-10 (bound 1e-9).
