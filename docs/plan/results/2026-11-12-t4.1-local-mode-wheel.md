# T4.1 local mode with the compiled expansion, from an installed wheel (2026-11-12)

Machine: local WSL2 box, AMD Ryzen 5 5600H (6 cores, 12 threads), Linux x86_64, CPU only.
Wheel: `pip wheel client/` against a local OpenFHE 1.6.1, `auditwheel repair`, installed in a clean
CPython 3.12 venv (no compiler on the path of the run, libraries from `ombra.libs`).
Preset m0_compact_n8192, 10,000 synthetic vectors, d = 768, 2 clusters, k = 10, median of 5
searches after one warm-up, top-1 was the source vector in 5/5.

Command: `[taskset -c 0-3] python tests/bench_local_mode.py --d 768`

| CPUs | create (s) | search median (s) | search runs (s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 logical (taskset 0-3) | 35.4 | 2.81 | 3.18, 2.81, 2.67, 2.56, 3.97 |
| 12 logical (all) | 53.0 | 2.71 | 3.97, 2.69, 2.43, 3.55, 2.71 |

The "cpus" field in the tool's JSON line shows 12 in both runs because it reports the machine's
count, not the taskset mask.

Before: the Python expansion (option A of decision 0022) took 27.4 s per search at d = 768 on
Modal (4 vCPU, 12 vectors, expansion alone). The compiled expansion measured 2.71 s to 2.81 s here
(T3.7 note: 2.71 s on 4 CPUs, 2.18 s on 8 CPUs). The machines differ, so read the ratio
(about 10x) as a rough figure.

Level check: `tests/wheel_check.py` on the installed wheel: the child process loads
`ombra._native` and not openfhe-python, and the score blocks come back at ciphertext level 1, the
server's level.
