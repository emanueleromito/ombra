# T3.11 follow-up: probe policies for filtered search (plaintext simulation)

- **What:** `core/bench/filter_probe_sim.py` (CPU only, numpy + faiss; no Modal, no encryption). JSON: `2026-11-11-t3.11-probe-policies.json`. Embeddings `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` on CPU, same loaders, cluster counts (arXiv 40,000 vectors / 20 clusters; banking77 10,003 / 6), queries (1,000 per cell) and filter cells as `2026-11-11-t3.11-filtered-recall.md`. Data fetched from the public URLs into a gitignored cache, never committed.
- **Why a simulation is valid:** T3.11 measured encrypted recall equal to plaintext recall under the same cluster choices (gap +0.0000 in all 21 cells). Here exact plaintext scores are used with the same planner code the client runs (`client/ombra/probe.py`). Check: policy A0 (decision 0019 as run in T3.11, stop at k matches) reproduces T3.11's recall in every cell (e.g. arXiv primary_category clustered 1%: 0.795; banking77 spread 1%: 0.755).
- **Ground truth:** brute-force exact filtered top-10 (float64). k = 10. After the policy's rounds, a query with fewer than k matches does a full (Strict) scan, as the client does.
- **Policies** (floor for all: the T3.11 first round, ceil(sqrt(clusters)) clusters = 5 for arXiv, 3 for banking77):
  - **E**: client-side per-cluster counts of each filter value. Clusters in centre-score order, clusters with zero expected matches skipped, add clusters until the expected match count reaches 3k (never fewer than the floor). Widen 2x only below k matches. Strict full scan up front if the expected selectivity over the collection is below 0.5%.
  - **E+A**: E, then the stopping check A: widen (2x per round, over clusters with nonzero expected matches, in centre-score order) while some remaining cluster has bound > the current k-th match score, at most 4 rounds. Bound for a cluster = `cos(max(0, theta_qc - theta_r))` with `theta_qc = arccos(centre score)` and `theta_r = arccos(min over members of cos(member, centre))` (the cluster radius as an angle; a valid upper bound on the score of any member by the triangle inequality on angles). A 1e-4 slack covers CKKS centre-score noise. Of the clusters that pass the bound, the next `n1 * 2^round` in centre order are searched (n1 = first round size).
  - **B**: baseline, decision 0019's rule but stopping at 3k matches: first round = floor, widen 2x up to 8x the floor, Strict fallback below k.
  - **A0**: decision 0019 unchanged (stop at k), for reference.
- **Modelled latency (not measured):** `latency = 71 ms + 10.8 ms x clusters searched + 0 ms x extra rounds`, a least-squares fit over the 20 cells of T3.11's measured mean latencies (RMSE 18 ms); the per-round term came out negative (clusters and rounds are correlated in T3.11's data) and was dropped to 0, so rounds are free in this model and the real cost of extra rounds is understated. A full scan is priced as all clusters. Treat the numbers as orders of magnitude.
- **Target:** recall@10 >= 0.95 at about 1% selectivity.

## Verdict at about 1% selectivity (target recall@10 >= 0.95)

| policy | cells at ~1% | min recall | meets 0.95 in all? | mean clusters at ~1% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A0 (0019, stop at k) | 6 | 0.7554 | NO | 6.1 |
| B (0019, stop at 3k) | 6 | 0.8296 | NO | 7.1 |
| E | 6 | 0.7682 | NO | 4.1 |
| E+A | 6 | 1.0000 | yes | 11.2 |

Reading: only E+A meets the target (recall 1.0000 in all six 1% cells; E+A is also 1.0000 in every 10% and 50% cell). The price is clusters: it searches 6 to 18 of 20 clusters on arXiv at 1% (18 on average for the spread filters, nearly a full scan) because the radius bound (min cosine to the centre) is loose for blurry 384-d text clusters, so the check rarely lets it stop early. E alone does not reach 0.95 (0.77 to 0.87 on the arXiv and banking77 spread cells): skipping empty clusters and a 3k first round are not enough when the true neighbours sit outside the first clusters. B is better than the 0019 rule but still 0.83 to 0.94. Latency figures are modelled and only meaningful as cluster counts. The bound is exact in plaintext; with CKKS the centre scores carry about 1e-5 noise, covered by the slack. A tighter radius (e.g. a high percentile with an exact fallback) would cut clusters but is not exact, and was not tried. Entirely unfiltered queries keep the basic rule (recall 0.983 and 0.993 as before).
