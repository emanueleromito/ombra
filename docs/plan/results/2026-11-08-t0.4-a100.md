# T0.4: Column-packed search throughput

- **Command:** `make bench TASK=t0.4`, then `make results-pull`
- **Commit:** `fa34522`
- **GPU:** NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB (as reported by CUDA)
- **CUDA:** 12.6.3 image, nvcc 12.6.85
- **OpenFHE:** 1.6.1
- **Parameter set:** `m0_depth2` from `core/params/ckks_presets.json` (decision 0009)
- **Timing:** median, min and max of 10 runs after 2 warm-up runs, CUDA events, kernel only

## Throughput

The kernel computes `sum_j stored_j * query_j` over d dimensions, ciphertext by ciphertext, with 8192 vectors per block.

## What these numbers exclude

- **Relinearization and rescale.** The result has 3 components, noise degree 2 and scale 2^100. Both operations are needed before a production search result, and they will lower real throughput.
- **Query expansion.** The query is already expanded; T0.5 measures that step.
- **Copies.** Transfer times are listed separately and are not part of vectors/s. The copy-to-GPU times include a host check that every word is reduced.

Blocks after the first are device-side copies of block 0 to avoid tens of thousands of CPU encryptions. Kernel time does not depend on the data values.
