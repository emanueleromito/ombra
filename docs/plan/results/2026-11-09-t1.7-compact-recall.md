`final_level` is 2 for every dataset. Timings come from the recall driver, which is built for measuring recall and scans all vectors per query; they are not a latency benchmark. T0.11 has the controlled expansion and key-size comparison. Blocks hold 8,192 vectors at `m0_depth2` and 4,096 at the compact preset, so the compact preset needs 28 blocks against 16 for the same 100,000 vectors plus padding; the stored-bytes difference is unused slots in partly filled blocks, not a saving per vector (T0.11: storage per vector is the same). More blocks is also why encryption (1.21x to 1.23x) and decryption (1.39x to 1.49x) take longer here than at `m0_depth2`, where the expansion is 1.6x to 2.1x faster.

## Limits

- Datasets are English text embeddings at 100,000 vectors; one embedding model per dimension.
- A pass says encrypted recall matches plaintext recall at the same cluster settings. It says nothing about the quality of clustering.
- The GPU pipeline here is the T0.8 one (expansion and search on the GPU; relinearize, rescale and mask in OpenFHE), as in T1.6.
- The exact GPU oracles for this preset are the existing ones. `tests/test_gpu_query_expansion.py::test_gpu_expansion_matches_openfhe[m0_compact_n8192-*]` still asserts 1e-6 and sometimes exceeds it (up to 1.470e-06); its exact-word checks are unaffected.
