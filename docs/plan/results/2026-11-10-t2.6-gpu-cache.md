# T2.6: GPU cluster cache, paging throughput and hit rate

- **Commands:** `modal run modal_app.py::test_gpu --pytest-args "tests/test_gpu_cache_paging.py -s"` (A10, exactness) and `modal run modal_app.py::bench --task t2.6` (A100-40GB, `m0_depth2`). Raw JSON: `t2.6-a100.json` (this folder).
- **Code:** `server/cache/` (pinned host pool, device arena with LRU, copy stream with events, `plan`/`next` double buffering); `ColumnSearchLauncher` in `core/` launches the unchanged column-search kernel at any device address.
- **Data:** synthetic seeded blocks (not real ciphertext contents); search is the column-search kernel only (no relinearization); the GPU cache starts cold for every timed run, the pinned pool is warm. Collection = 3x the GPU budget (24 blocks, budget 8).

## Done-when

- **Same results as everything resident:** passed on both presets (`m0_depth2`, `m0_compact_n8192`) on the A10: every output RNS word equals the all-resident run, over two random-order passes with a budget of one third of the collection; evictions 14, hits 1, hit rate 0.056; a lease held during eviction pressure survived and a budget below one block was refused. Hard rule 3: the kernel is unchanged; the test shows paging does not corrupt data.
- **Paging throughput and hit rate:** below.

## Host to device bandwidth (A100, PCIe)

| Block size | pinned GB/s | pageable GB/s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 MiB | 19.0 to 19.6 | 19.4 to 21.0 |
| 16 MiB | 21.9 to 23.2 | 17.7 to 18.4 |
| 64 MiB | 24.0 to 25.4 | 13.9 to 17.2 |
| 256 MiB | 24.6 to 25.0 | 13.3 to 15.7 |

(range over the three dimensions; pinned memory gives 1.6x to 1.9x at 64 to 256 MiB.) One cache block is a whole cluster block: 12.6 MB at d = 16, 50.3 MB at d = 64, 201 MB at d = 256.

## Search over a collection 3x the GPU budget (96 accesses, median of 5)

| d | serial s | overlapped s | compute only s | overlap speedup | paging GB/s (overlapped) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 0.0466 | 0.0464 | 0.0022 | 1.005x | 24.7 |
| 64 | 0.1733 | 0.1726 | 0.0095 | 1.004x | 26.5 |
| 256 | 0.7727 | 0.8160 | 0.0354 | 0.947x | 22.5 |

**Reading:** the search is entirely paging-bound. Compute is 4.8% to 5.5% of the time, and paging runs at 22 to 26 GB/s, the PCIe limit measured above, so there is almost nothing to overlap and copy/compute overlap gives no gain (1.00x; 0.95x at d = 256, where one copy stream and larger blocks make it slightly worse). The cache's value is hit rate, not overlap: every hit avoids a 25 GB/s transfer.

## Hit rate (384 accesses, budget = 1/3 of the collection)

| Access pattern | hit rate | time at d = 256 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| uniform | 0.315 | 2.19 s |
| zipf, s = 1 | 0.583 | 1.29 s |
| random order, 2 passes (test) | 0.052 | |

The hit rate is the same at all three dimensions (it depends on the access pattern and the budget, not on block size). A skewed workload (popular clusters) nearly doubles it and cuts time by 41%.

## Limits

- Overlap was not shown to help with this kernel; it may with heavier per-block compute (relinearize, mask, rescale in T2.7), which should be re-measured there.
- Synthetic blocks and a synthetic access pattern; real hit rates depend on the query workload and the cluster choice (Decoy mode adds random clusters).
- Pinned pool 3x the GPU budget at d = 256 is 4.8 GB of host memory.
- The loader in the test reads ciphertext files, not storage segments; wiring it to T2.5 segments is part of T2.7.
- A single run on one A100; the pinned bandwidth varies about 5% between dimensions.
