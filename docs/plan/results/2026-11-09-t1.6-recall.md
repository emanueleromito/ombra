## The check

One command: `modal run modal_app.py::recall [--preset NAME] [--dataset "NAME ..."|all] [--n N] [--queries Q] [--dry-run]`. Locally: `python core/bench/recall.py` with the same options. The rule is in `core/bench/recall_check.py`.

A preset **passes** if, on at least 2 public embedding datasets with at least 1,000 queries each, recall@10 under encryption (no re-ranking) is within 0.005 of the plaintext recall at the same cluster settings, for every setting: the full scan and each nlist x nprobe. Score error is reported as a safety margin only. A run that is too small to count is `NOT ELIGIBLE`.

The pipeline is the real encrypted one at level 2 (as T0.8): GPU expansion and search, then relinearize, rescale, encrypted all-ones mask and rescale in OpenFHE, then client decryption. The encrypted scan runs once per query over all vectors, and decrypted scores are restricted to the probed clusters.

## Result

**PASS on `m0_depth2`, on all 3 datasets, with 1,000 queries each. The worst recall gap is 0.0000 at every one of the 22 settings per dataset.**

The 768-dimensional dataset is a derived copy, because the original `Cohere/wikipedia-22-12-simple-embeddings` is no longer reachable. Datasets stored as streams use the first 2 x (N + queries) rows as their pool, so that pool is the start of the file, not a sample of the whole. Database (100,000 vectors) and queries (1,000) are disjoint seeded splits of the pool, normalized.

Entries below 1e-9 are flushed to zero, because OpenFHE refuses to encode a column of values around 1e-32 ("Scaling factor too small"). `wiki-simple-minilm-384` has 2 such dimensions, which accounts for its 202,000 flushed entries across 101,000 rows. Rows that are zero, non-finite or exact duplicates are dropped.
