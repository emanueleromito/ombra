# Licensing

Ombra is **source-available**. The full code is public, and you can use it under one of two licenses:

1. the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE), free for non-commercial use, or
2. a **commercial license**, for any use that the noncommercial terms do not cover.

You only need one of them. The files in this repository carry the SPDX identifier `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0 OR LicenseRef-Ombra-Commercial`.

## Option 1: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (free)

In plain words, you may use, copy, modify and share Ombra for any noncommercial purpose, as long as you keep the license terms and the `Required Notice:` line from [LICENSE](LICENSE) with every copy. The full legal text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) is what counts; this is only a summary.

| Who | Free? | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individuals, hobbyists, students | Yes | Nothing |
| Universities and research institutes | Yes | Nothing |
| Charities, public bodies, non-commercial organizations | Yes | Nothing |
| Companies evaluating Ombra before buying | Yes, for a limited trial | A free evaluation license |
| Any company using Ombra for business, including internally for its own AI, products or services | No | A paid commercial license |

What counts as commercial: any use that is aimed at commercial advantage or private monetary compensation, for example running Ombra in a product or service you sell, or inside a company for its own business operations. Personal use, research, education and use by charities and public bodies are noncommercial.

## Option 2: Commercial license

A commercial license replaces the PolyForm Noncommercial terms for the company that signs it. It is sold per company or per production cluster, on a yearly basis, with an optional paid support tier.
