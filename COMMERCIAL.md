# Commercial licensing

Ombra is **source-available**, not open source. The full code is public, but the license depends on who you are.

| Who | Free? | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individuals, hobbyists, students | Yes | Nothing, the [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) terms apply |
| Universities and research institutes | Yes | Nothing |
| Charities, public bodies, non-commercial organizations | Yes | Nothing |
| Companies evaluating Ombra before buying | Yes, for a limited trial | A free evaluation license |
| Any company using Ombra for business, including internally for its own AI, products or services | No | A paid commercial license |

A commercial license replaces the PolyForm Noncommercial terms for the company that signs it. It is sold per company or per production cluster, on a yearly basis, with an optional paid support tier.

## How to get a license

Open an issue titled "Commercial license" or write to Erai Labs at emanueleromito@erailabs.com. Tell us your company, your intended use and roughly how large your deployment is.

## Contributions

Outside contributors sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) before their first pull request is merged, so their code can be included in commercial licenses.
